Support & Transparency

Parent's Admissions FAQ

Answering critical questions about security, cutoffs, and post-secondary choices for Canadian families.

How secure is my teenager's academic data?

Pathway Canada enforces a Zero-Password Architecture. We do not store passwords; logins are managed through secure enterprise social auth providers. Additionally, we isolate Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from academic metrics, hosting all records on secure Canadian servers compliant with PIPEDA and Bill C-27.

Why should my child use this matching tool?

Canadian families spend over $550 on average in redundant college and university application fees. Pathway Canada optimizes submissions by comparing grades and course prerequisites against strict program cutoffs, directing applications to programs where success is statistically probable.

Are regional colleges and universities supported?

Absolutely. We track undergraduate programs, professional diplomas, and certificate courses across all 13 Canadian provinces and territories. Families can filter search profiles by province to discover local options and tuition savings.

How do we contact support or get help?

We are here to support your family. If you have questions regarding database options, cutoffs, or methodologies, please email our support desk directly at support@pathwayscanada.ca or use the conversational advisor widget below.
